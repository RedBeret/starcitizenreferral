Quick answer

Plan a route in the map, select the appropriate navigation and quantum operating mode, align with the target, wait for calibration, then engage. If travel will not begin, identify which of those stages is incomplete.

Set a destination before changing flight modes

Open the map, find a location by name, and inspect its route information before committing to a long journey. RSI’s quantum-travel guide documents the map search, route selection, required fuel and intermediate jumps.

For your first attempt, choose a destination you can identify again if you need to reopen the map. Write down the final destination and inspect the next route leg; those are not always the same thing.

The sequence documented by RSI

  1. Open the map. RSI lists F2 as the default map shortcut.
  2. Select a destination and set the route. Review the fuel and route details.
  3. Enter Navigation mode. The cited guide lists B, then middle-mouse to select the QT operating mode.
  4. Align with the selected target. Watch the calibration state.
  5. Engage when ready. The documented default is left-mouse after calibration completes.

These are defaults from RSI’s published guide, not a promise that every custom profile or future patch uses identical inputs. If the controls behave differently, match the actions to your current in-game keybindings and HUD. Pressing a familiar key repeatedly is less useful than checking the ship’s actual state.

Quantum drive not working? Check the stage

What you seeFirst thing to inspect
No useful targetThe route and selected destination in the map.
The wrong ship modeThe NAV/QT indicators and the action bound to changing modes.
Calibration unfinishedAlignment with the current route leg and the calibration display.
A route you cannot followFuel information, intermediate legs and whether the destination is appropriate from your position.

Change one thing at a time and look for a changed indicator. If none of the states respond, record your ship, environment and visible message before checking current known issues. This checklist is a way to narrow the problem; it does not establish that every failed jump is a player mistake.

Travelling around a planet

Orbital travel, a surface destination and the final manual landing are different parts of a trip. The official guide describes planetside markers and quantum approaches. After arrival, leave time to orient yourself and request landing clearance where required.

A first trip is easier to follow when you already know how to retrieve the ship and return to a spaceport. Use the New Babbage first-flight route to practise those parts separately.

Meeting a friend

RSI also documents selecting a friend or party member in the map as a route target. Coordinate the meeting location first so the target is not moving while you are trying to understand the route.

If you cannot find each other at all, start with the contacts and party checklist. Being on a friends list, being grouped, and standing at the same location are separate checks.
