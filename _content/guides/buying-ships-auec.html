Quick answer

Visit an in-game ship dealer, inspect its terminal, select the exact ship and review the aUEC purchase confirmation. After buying, retrieve it through ASOP. An in-game purchase is subject to alpha persistence and is separate from an RSI website pledge.

Find a dealer and inspect its terminal

RSI’s buying guide names New Deal in Lorville and Astro Armada in Area18 as dealer examples. It also explains that the showroom floor does not display every available ship. Use the dealer’s terminal to inspect its selection.

Check the ship and variant name carefully. Similar names and silhouettes can conceal differences relevant to the job you want to do. Keep the intended task in mind when browsing.

Understand the balance you are spending

aUEC is the alpha’s in-game currency. It is distinct from account UEC, Arena Commander REC and website store credit. RSI’s currency documentation describes those separate uses and explains that alpha balances can change or reset.

Plan around the balance shown in the game. Do not treat a screenshot of a website account balance as proof that you can afford the current dealer price.

Review the purchase before confirming

  1. Check the exact model and variant.
  2. Read the current terminal price and your available balance.
  3. Leave room in your plan for equipment, services and the next activity.
  4. Read the confirmation screen before accepting the purchase.
  5. Retrieve the ship through ASOP and note the assigned hangar.

The dealer documentation says ships cannot be sold back and support cannot reverse these purchases. Take the confirmation step seriously rather than relying on an undo option.

When renting first is useful

A rental can help answer practical questions before a larger aUEC purchase. Try getting into the cockpit, taking off, landing and performing the intended task. Check rental availability and component restrictions before planning that trial.

A purchase makes more sense when you understand what it adds to your sessions. A larger ship can introduce extra boarding, cargo or crew steps; compare the actual workflow with the time and people you usually have available.

Ownership and alpha persistence

A ship acquired inside the alpha does not become a website pledge. Read current patch and persistence notices before assuming it will carry through a future reset. This guide promises neither permanent retention nor a particular wipe schedule.

If retrieval is the confusing part after a successful purchase, follow the hangar and ASOP guide. To work toward a purchase, use the mission checklist to choose manageable work rather than assuming every advertised payout is guaranteed.
