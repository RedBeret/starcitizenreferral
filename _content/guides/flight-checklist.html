Reusable departure checklist

Tick each item after checking it in the game, or mark it when you have decided it does not apply. This is a planning aid for a trip, not a guarantee of safety or an automatic check of your ship.

The list avoids fixed keys, ship rankings and fuel quantities. It follows the general preparation described in RSI’s first-flight instructions and maintenance guide, with additional planning suggestions from this site.

Ticks last until you reset or reload this page. Nothing is saved to an account. A checked box means you reviewed the item yourself.

1. Decide on the trip

Purpose and return plan

If you are choosing work, read the mission offer checklist before committing. For a learning session, one short trip with a clear outcome can be enough.

2. Check the character and equipment

Equipment and recovery

Check item locations rather than assuming equipment in another city has travelled with you. Use the medical guide when you need to understand regeneration.

3. Check the ship

Retrieval and readiness

For a service request, inspect its result rather than assuming the confirmation alone means every resource is full. The maintenance walkthrough explains the screens.

4. Coordinate and depart

Crew, clearance and movement

Pause at the last item until you can observe the actual exit. Completing the list is not an instruction to move through a closed door. For a group trip, use the party guide to arrange the meeting first.

Use it again after the trip

Reset the ticks for the next departure. If an important step was unclear, follow its linked guide and practise that step separately. You can print the list with your browser’s print command; the print button offers the same action when JavaScript is available.

The checklist is deliberately a prompt to look and think. It cannot read your game, verify a crew member’s status or calculate whether the chosen ship can complete a route.
