Quick answer

For the Persistent Universe, use a ship rental terminal and pay the displayed aUEC price for the selected term. The rental clock starts immediately and continues while you are offline. Arena Commander uses a separate REC rental system.

Decide what the rental needs to prove

Use the ship comparison framework to set up a useful trial. Before comparing hulls, choose a task: learning a cockpit, trying a different flight style, or checking whether a ship suits a mission. A focused trial makes it easier to decide whether to rent again or consider an in-game purchase.

RSI’s rental documentation distinguishes the Persistent Universe and Arena Commander. Check which mode a recommendation refers to before travelling to a terminal.

Rent in the Persistent Universe

  1. Find a rental terminal at a major spaceport.
  2. Inspect the ships actually offered there.
  3. Select the exact ship and rental duration.
  4. Review the displayed aUEC charge and confirm only when ready to use it.
  5. Use an ASOP terminal to retrieve the rental and note its assignment.

Build the session around the rental rather than purchasing a term immediately before logging out. RSI states that the timer starts on rental and continues offline. Persistent Universe rental components also cannot be customized under the documented rental rules.

The official currency guide explains the different balances. Website store credit is another distinct balance, not a substitute for the aUEC charge at a universe rental terminal.

Use the trial deliberately

Start with departure and landing, then attempt the activity you wanted to evaluate. Make notes about visibility, access to the cargo area, controls and the practical steps of the task. These observations are more useful than judging only the ship’s appearance.

Do not assume a rental’s fixed equipment represents every possible customized setup. Equally, do not assume a video using modified components shows the rental configuration you will receive.

Check availability and cost at the terminal

Ship selections, terms and prices can change. This guide intentionally uses the current terminal as the decision point instead of an undated price table. Keep enough aUEC for equipment and the rest of your planned session.
