Quick answer

Add each other as contacts, form a party, then coordinate a shared game session and meeting place. A friend request, party invitation and shared contract each do a different job.

Before you arrange the session

Check that everyone has finished updating the same game environment and can launch it. An existing friend on a different test channel is not a useful starting point for a public LIVE session. The launcher guide explains the environment choice.

Agree on a small first objective: meet at one named station, retrieve one ship, or take a short flight. Trying to explain account setup, transit, flight controls and a difficult contract at once makes it harder to tell which step someone has missed.

Add each other as contacts

RSI’s friends guide describes the main-menu Friends bar, Spectrum contacts, and in-game contact options. Exchange the actual RSI handles you are searching for, not just a nickname used in another chat application.

  1. Locate the other player through the supported contacts interface.
  2. Check whether the request has been accepted.
  3. Confirm that both players appear in the expected contact list before attempting the next step.

Form the party and choose a meeting point

From the main menu, contacts can use the available join and group options. In-game, RSI documents inviting contacts through mobiGlas Commlink, with F11 listed as its default shortcut. Check the active keybinding if your profile differs.

Read back a precise rendezvous: the system, planet or station, and a particular terminal or entrance. “I’m at the spaceport” can still leave two people at different cities, platforms or hangars. If one person is still in a hab, let them finish the transit route before moving the target again.

For a new player at microTech, the New Babbage route gives a common set of landmarks. For a meeting away from the city, use the quantum-travel checklist.

Share the contract separately

A party is not the same thing as everyone accepting a mission. RSI’s contract guide describes sharing a selected accepted mission and having the other members accept it. It also explains that the shared reward is divided among the accepting party members.

Before departing, ask each participant to confirm that the mission appears in their accepted list. Then check the objective and destination together. The beginner mission guide includes a short pre-departure checklist.

No regular group yet? Use the official Guide System

The RSI Guide System helps learners request an experienced player for particular topics. Select a skill you actually want help with, such as basic flight or missions, so the session has a clear purpose.

A useful request is specific: “I can launch the game but need help reaching my ship and making a first landing.” Tell the guide your starting location and available time. That gives them something concrete to plan around without asking for private account details.
