Quick answer

If you start in The Nest, follow the Metroloop signs to the Spaceport line, retrieve your ship at an ASOP terminal, and note the assigned hangar. If you start in a personal hangar, begin with the terminal already near you.

First, work out where you spawned

New Babbage is the city on microTech in the Stanton system. Your first steps depend on whether you followed the tutorial and where the game placed you. Do not leave a personal hangar to search for a hotel just because a walkthrough begins in a bed.

RSI’s getting-started guide explains the tutorial and personal-hangar starting paths. Follow active tutorial objectives if they differ from this free-flight route.

From The Nest to your ship

  1. Reach the lobby. Leave the hab and use the elevator to the ground floor.
  2. Find the Metroloop. Follow the transit signs outside and down to the platforms.
  3. Choose the Spaceport line. Check the destination signs before boarding. The Commons or Expo route is a different trip.
  4. Enter the spaceport. At the Spaceport stop, take the elevators up and pass through the security route toward departures.
  5. Find an ASOP terminal. Use the ship-retrieval console and record the assigned hangar or your personal-hangar entry.
  6. Take the correct elevator. Select the destination assigned to your ship, then board it.

The official illustrated New Babbage directions show the relevant signs and consoles. Use them when a landmark is unfamiliar. A named destination is more dependable than counting turns while distracted.

Already in a personal hangar?

Use the nearby ASOP terminal to deliver or retrieve the ship you intend to fly. Read its status and wait for the retrieval process to complete. Know which ship you selected before approaching a cockpit; a list entry and an available vehicle in the hangar are different checkpoints.

For a first session, keep the objective small: board, identify the flight controls, and make a controlled departure. Shopping, a long-distance route and your first contract can wait until you can reliably retrieve and return the ship.

Prepare for takeoff

  • Use the ship’s interaction prompts to find its entrance and pilot seat.
  • Read the cockpit state and power or flight-ready controls. Check your in-game keybindings rather than assuming an older shortcut still applies.
  • Request departure clearance and wait for the assigned exit to open.
  • Make small inputs and check the flight display before accelerating away.

RSI’s flight instructions are linked below. If you are still learning the controls, a guided session is a useful next step; playing with someone experienced can make the first departure easier.

Practise returning before taking a long contract

At a serviced landing zone, contact Landing Services or Air Traffic Control, follow the assigned marker, and lower the landing gear before landing. Watch the clearance and hangar orientation rather than aiming at the city skyline.

RSI’s landing guide covers authorization and approach controls. Once the local trip feels manageable, move on to quantum travel or a carefully chosen first mission.
