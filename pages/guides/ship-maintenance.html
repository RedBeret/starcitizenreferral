The pilot’s field guide

Repair, refuel and rearm your Star Citizen ship.

Finish the landing first, choose the services you need, then confirm the ship’s displayed state before setting off again.

By PatchOpsSources checked 15 November 2026Independent community guide
Quick answer

Land at a facility offering services, stay in the pilot seat, and open Landing Services in mobiGlas. Select the work you need, review the request and confirm it. Check the ship’s displays again before departure.

Find a serviced landing location

RSI’s vehicle maintenance instructions describe repair, restock and refuelling at landing locations. Service availability varies; arriving beside an outpost is not confirmation that its pad can provide everything you need.

Complete the ATC and landing sequence before opening the service screen. When planning a trip, identify somewhere to stop before the fuel warning becomes your only navigation plan.

Request repair, refuel or restock

  1. Remain seated in the landed ship’s pilot seat.
  2. Open mobiGlas; RSI documents F1 as the default binding.
  3. Choose Landing Services and inspect the available options.
  4. Select the required services and review the displayed request.
  5. Confirm, allow the operation to finish, and check the resulting ship state.

Treat the confirmation screen as a checkpoint. If an option is unavailable, read its state before repeatedly submitting the same request.

Hydrogen and quantum fuel do different jobs

ResourcePurposeTrip check
Hydrogen fuelThruster movement.Enough for departure, approach and local manoeuvring.
Quantum fuelQuantum travel.Enough for the planned route and a sensible next stop.

Checking one gauge does not answer both questions. Read the fuel labels rather than assuming any full bar means the ship is ready for a long journey. The quantum travel walkthrough covers route preparation separately.

When services are missing or appear incomplete

Work through the prerequisites in order: correct facility, completed landing, pilot seat, correct mobiGlas page, then service availability. Compare the request result with the ship’s displayed fuel, damage and ammunition state. Note which service failed rather than treating them as a single operation.

If the interface and ship disagree, keep a record of the location, ship and exact service attempted. This guide does not promise a universal workaround for alpha service bugs.

A short departure check

  • Fuel types checked separately.
  • Requested repair or restock result inspected.
  • Next destination and landing plan understood.
  • Any remaining fault considered before taking a mission.

Routine servicing and an insurance claim solve different problems. Read the relevant process before abandoning a ship or assuming a claim will recover its contents.
