Medical gameplay and respawning in Star Citizen.

A guide to the game’s medical systems: assess the character state, choose appropriate help, and understand where you may return.

By PatchOpsSources checked 15 November 2026Independent community guide
Quick answer

In Star Citizen, restoring health, treating an injury and setting a regeneration point are different actions. Check the character’s condition and the facility’s capabilities rather than assuming that a full health bar resolves every problem.

Read the in-game character state

RSI’s medical gameplay guide distinguishes health damage, injury impairments, incapacitation and regeneration. This article concerns those fictional game systems.

For your next decision, identify whether the character can still move, whether an injury remains, and what help is available nearby. Tell a teammate what the interface shows rather than describing every problem as simply “low health.”

Treatment capabilities are different

Game injury levelTreatment described by RSI
MinorAppropriate field treatment or a Tier 3 medical bed.
ModerateCan be stabilized, but full healing requires Tier 2 or better.
SevereCan be stabilized, but full healing requires Tier 1 or a hospital.

Stabilization can suppress an impairment without fully resolving its cause. Check the treatment result and remaining status before continuing the activity that led to the injury.

Incapacitated: request help and give a location

The official guide documents a medical assistance beacon, with holding M as the default on a QWERTY layout. Other layouts or custom bindings can differ. Use the current prompt and read any displayed reward or cost.

If you are already with a group, tell them your location and character state. A useful report includes the ship or facility, whether you are inside or outside, and the last reachable landmark. The party guide helps establish the contacts and shared location before a trip.

Set and verify a regeneration point

RSI describes selecting a suitable medical bed as a regeneration location. Availability also depends on that bed functioning, being in range and, where applicable, having MedGel. Read the facility’s current options rather than assuming every bed provides identical services.

After setting a point, make a note of the actual facility or ship. Changing the regeneration location does not transport spare armor or supplies there. The inventory guide explains how local storage remains tied to its location.

Make a simple recovery plan

  1. Location: identify where you expect to return.
  2. Supplies: know where a spare basic loadout is stored.
  3. Transport: decide how to reach your next destination.
  4. Group: tell teammates if your plans or location changed.

RSI notes that belongings may be left at the place of death. Returning does not guarantee they will still be recoverable. Decide whether a retrieval attempt is practical before turning one failed trip into another long journey.

For a first session, concentrate on learning these locations and options. You can build a more elaborate rescue or medical playstyle once the basic interfaces make sense.
