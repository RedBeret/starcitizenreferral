The 50,000 UEC bonus, explained.

Know what the standard referral reward is, what the current signup form says, and how credits differ during alpha testing.

By PatchOpsReviewed 15 November 2026Independent community guide

What is the standard bonus?

RSI advertises a 50,000 UEC referral benefit for new accounts using a valid player code. The code on this site is STAR-9L66-ZTDY. This is the standard benefit, not an exclusive rate or a checkout discount.

When are the credits available?

The support FAQ associates the benefit with registering through a referral. The current signup form describes receiving the credits after buying a game package. Follow the wording shown on RSI when you sign up; we do not promise instantly spendable credits without a purchase.

UEC, aUEC and store credit

These are different balances. UEC is the currency intended for the released game. aUEC is used for alpha testing and can be reset. Store credit is a separate Pledge Store balance.

RSI’s currency guide explains how account UEC and patch testing rules affect starting alpha balances. Package-included UEC is treated separately. There is no single guaranteed starting aUEC total to quote for every player or patch.

A credit bonus is not a free game package. It does not reduce the checkout price. Check the current package and event terms on RSI.

Forgot to enter the code?

If your account is under 24 hours old and has no referral attached, open the RSI account summary from the user icon and look for the referral field near the bottom. A late-added code may take longer to credit. An existing referral association cannot be changed.
