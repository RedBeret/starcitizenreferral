Find your hangar and retrieve your ship.

Separate the hangar at your home city from a temporary spaceport assignment and the website’s My Hangar account page.

By PatchOpsSources checked 15 November 2026Independent community guide
Quick answer

Your personal hangar is at your primary residence’s spaceport. Away from home, the spaceport provides a temporary public hangar. Request your ship through ASOP and follow the specific assignment it gives you.

Three meanings of “hangar”

NameMeaning
Personal hangarThe in-game hangar associated with your primary residence.
Public hangarA spaceport-managed assignment for use away from home or when a ship needs another space.
My Hangar on the RSI websiteYour account’s pledge list; it is not an elevator destination in the game.

RSI’s hangar FAQ explains how residence, assignments and ship retrieval fit together. Start by checking where your character currently is, rather than looking for a personal hangar at every station.

Retrieve a ship and read the assignment

  1. Go to the spaceport and find an ASOP terminal.
  2. Select the intended ship and use the available delivery or retrieval action.
  3. Read where the terminal sent it.
  4. Follow the elevator destination matching that assignment.
  5. Check the ship name before boarding and preparing departure.

There is also an ASOP terminal near the elevators inside a personal hangar. Keep clear of the marked main floor while the ship is being brought in. For a complete city route, use the New Babbage first-flight guide.

Hangar size and larger ships

The documented initial allocation uses the largest ship in your pledged fleet. RSI explains that obtaining a larger ship after allocation does not automatically enlarge the existing personal hangar; a public hangar can handle an assignment that does not fit.

Read the actual terminal result. A different assignment is not, by itself, evidence that your ship is missing.

Friends, equipment and local storage

Party members can visit your in-game hangar. Agree on the city, server and meeting point before trying to board together; the friends guide covers that coordination.

Finding the right hangar is separate from finding the right equipment. Consult the inventory guide for personal, local and ship storage. Do not assume gear stored at another city has followed you home.

When the hangar or ship seems missing

Check these facts in order: your current location, whether it is your primary residence, the ship selected in ASOP, its displayed state, and the latest assignment. Returning to an old elevator destination without checking the terminal can send you to the wrong place.

Ground vehicles introduce another distinction: spawning a vehicle does not mean every hangar offers a usable ground exit. Plan how it will leave before retrieving it. For an arriving ship, the separate landing guide explains requesting clearance and following an ATC assignment.
