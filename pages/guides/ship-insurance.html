Star Citizen ship insurance, claims and LTI.

Separate the ship you need back today from the insurance listed on a pledge and plans for the finished game.

By PatchOpsSources checked 15 November 2026Independent community guide
Quick answer

A pledge’s insurance duration, today’s alpha replacement process and custom-loadout coverage are different things. RSI says the insurance period included in a pledge starts after testing ends, not when you first buy the package.

Start with the question you are trying to answer

Your questionWhat to check
My ship is missing nowIts current ASOP status, claim options and known issues.
What does my purchase include?The insurance explicitly listed on that pledge.
Will modified equipment return?The current loadout-insurance rules and insured configuration.

Keeping those questions separate prevents a common misunderstanding: a line saying “six months” in a pledge is not a six-month countdown that began the day you installed the alpha.

How insurance works during alpha testing

The official insurance FAQ describes basic alpha/PTU coverage without a recurring upkeep charge. Claim or expedite costs may still apply. Read the actual terminal before confirming a replacement.

If you are stranded, identify the ship and its location first. Retrieving an available ship, travelling back to it, and filing a replacement claim are different actions. Treat the confirmation screen as a checkpoint rather than clicking through it while trying to get back into a mission.

Check a claim before confirming

RSI documents filing claims through an ASOP terminal: select the relevant ship, inspect its status, and use the claim confirmation. Its FAQ also advises handling an included vehicle before the parent ship where applicable.

For a practical checklist, record the ship, displayed status, expected wait, cost and any equipment you changed. If the option is absent or produces an error, check current known issues before repeating the same action. Our retrieval walkthrough explains where the terminal fits into a normal departure.

Custom loadouts need their own check

RSI’s current FAQ describes an Insure Loadout option and returning the last insured equipment configuration after a claim. Review that configuration after making changes. Do not assume that insuring an earlier setup also recorded equipment fitted later.

The question is what the current system says is insured, not simply whether you remember using the insurance option before. Keep cargo, personal belongings and ship-mounted equipment distinct when reading the available coverage.

What does Lifetime Insurance mean?

RSI describes LTI as coverage that does not require ongoing UEC payments to maintain. It does not remove claim or repair costs. Check the individual pledge for the coverage it actually includes; the referral code on this site does not add LTI.

When choosing a first game package, compare game access, contents, price and insurance as separate lines. Read the current official listing before making a purchase decision, especially if the listing comes from an old event or promotion.

Separate current mechanics from future plans

Insurance is part of an unfinished game, and coverage details can change during development. Use current RSI documentation for the specific question in front of you. A forum explanation of a future system should not be treated as a tested description of today’s claim screen.
