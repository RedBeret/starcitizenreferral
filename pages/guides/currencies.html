Understand Star Citizen’s different currencies.

Begin with the currency label and where you are using it. That distinction is more useful than comparing two unexplained numbers.

By PatchOpsSources checked 15 November 2026Independent community guide
Quick answer

aUEC, UEC, REC and store credit are different balances. Identify the currency label and where you are spending it before comparing amounts. A balance on the website does not automatically describe the credits available in your current game session.

The four balances at a glance

RSI’s currency reference explains the distinctions:

CurrencyMain contextWhat to check
aUECAlpha gameplay in the Persistent Universe.The balance displayed inside the game.
UECAccount currency intended for the released game.Account UEC and any separately listed package contents.
RECArena Commander equipment and ship rentals.The rental mode and eligible equipment.
Store creditEligible pledges through the RSI website.The website checkout and its payment conditions.

These labels are part of the transaction, not decoration. Write down the label alongside the number if you are asking someone to help explain a balance.

Why aUEC and UEC can differ

aUEC is temporary alpha testing currency; its persistence and starting amounts depend on testing decisions. Earning or spending it does not change the UEC intended for release. Package-included UEC is also listed separately from the website’s account UEC balance in RSI’s explanation.

For a missing-credit question, collect three facts: which screen you are reading, its currency label, and the source of the amount you expected. A screenshot of one balance and a description of another can make an ordinary distinction look like a failed transaction.

Rental credits depend on the mode

RSI’s rental guide distinguishes aUEC rentals in the Persistent Universe from REC rentals in Arena Commander. The ship you can rent in one mode is not automatically available through the other.

Before following a rental recommendation, ask where the person rented it. Then compare the terminal or mode you are actually using. The rental walkthrough covers duration and retrieval.

Website store credit has a separate purpose

Store credit is used toward eligible website pledges and can arise from reclaiming pledges or redeeming gift cards. It is not the aUEC wallet used at an in-game ship dealer.

As a practical example, if you are standing at a dealer terminal, check your in-game aUEC and the dealer’s displayed price. If you are on a website checkout, read that checkout’s accepted payment conditions. Comparing the two totals without their context will not answer whether either transaction can complete.

A reusable balance checklist

  • Where: website, Persistent Universe or Arena Commander?
  • Which label: aUEC, UEC, REC or store credit?
  • Why expected: mission reward, package contents, account grant or reclaimed pledge?
  • Which environment: the same game environment in which you earned it?
